Company Travel Policy

Employees traveling for official work must book travel through the approved corporate
travel portal at least 5 working days before departure.

Domestic travel: Economy class flights only, unless travel duration exceeds 6 hours,
in which case premium economy may be approved by the reporting manager.

International travel requires VP-level approval regardless of duration.

Per diem for domestic travel is set at 1500 INR per day, covering meals and local transport.
International per diem varies by country and is listed in Annexure B.

Any leave taken immediately before or after official travel (for personal reasons) must
be requested separately through the standard annual leave process and is not covered
under travel policy.
